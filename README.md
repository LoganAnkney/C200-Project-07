## What did you 
The main issue was I decided to change my game in around the last week timeframe, thinking of the idea, and thought I would hve more fun, which I did. Originally I was trying for one of the "Rage games" I've been seeing recently, and thought it would be fun as a one button you want to press, but being unable to. But I wanted to really do something with music, and I was able to create this final game with music heavily inspiring it and me. 
I struggled with getting the scenes and death areas to die, falling off or not comepleting in time. I will definitely be add a few more levels to make it much more difficult, and expansive. Making different score mixes and such.
I was surprised at how long it took to add parts and sections into the "Flight of the Bumblebee" "Remix", that I made, thought it would honestly not take long, but it took me a few days, going through different additions and added sections until I was actually happy with it, and still keeping the bumblebee.
